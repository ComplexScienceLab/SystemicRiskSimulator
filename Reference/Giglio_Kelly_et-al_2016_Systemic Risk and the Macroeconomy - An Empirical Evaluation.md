### Abstract

 This article studies how systemic risk and ﬁnancial market distress affect the distribution of shocks to real economic activity. We analyze how changes in 19 different measures of systemic risk skew the distribution of subsequent shocks to industrial production and other macroeconomic variables in the US and Europe over several decades. We also propose dimension reduction estimators for constructing systemic risk indexes from the cross section of measures and demonstrate their success in predicting future macroeconomic shocks out of sample.



## 摘要

本文研究系统性风险和金融市场困境如何影响对实际经济活动的冲击的分布。我们分析了几十种系统风险的19种不同度量方法的变化如何使随后的冲击分布在美国和欧洲的工业生产和其他宏观经济变量上。我们还提出了降维估计量，用于从度量的各个方面构建系统风险指标，并证明了它们在预测样本中未来的宏观经济冲击方面的成功。
