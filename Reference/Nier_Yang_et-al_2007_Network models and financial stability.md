## Abstract

 Systemic risk is a key concern for central banks charged with safeguarding overall ﬁnancial stability. In this paper we investigate how systemic risk is affected by the structure of the ﬁnancial system. We construct banking systems that are composed of a number of banks that are connected by interbank linkages. We then vary the key parameters that deﬁne the structure of the ﬁnancial system – including its level of capitalisation, the degree to which banks are connected, the size of interbank exposures and the degree of concentration of the system – and analyse the inﬂuence of these parameters on the likelihood of knock-on defaults. First, we ﬁnd that the better capitalised banks are, the more resilient is the banking system against contagious defaults and this effect is non-linear. Second, the effect of the degree of connectivity is non-monotonic, i.e. initially a small increase in connectivity increases the contagion effect; but after a certain threshold value, connectivity improves the ability of a banking system to absorb shocks. Third, the size of interbank liabilities tends to increase the risk of knock-on default, even if banks hold capital against such exposures. Fourth, more concentrated banking systems are shown to be prone to larger systemic risk, all else equal. In an extension to the main analysis we study how liquidity effects interact with banking structure to produce a greater chance of systemic breakdown. We ﬁnally consider how the risk of contagion might depend on the degree of asymmetry (tiering) inherent in the structure of the banking system. A number of our results have important implications for public policy, which this paper also draws out.





# 摘要



系统性风险是负责维护整体金融稳定的中央银行的主要关切。在本文中，我们研究了金融系统结构如何影响系统性风险。我们构建的银行系统由许多银行之间的银行间联系所组成。然后，我们改变定义金融系统结构的关键参数，包括其资本化水平，银行的连接程度，银行间敞口的规模以及系统的集中度，并分析这些参数的影响连锁违约的可能性。首先，我们发现资本充足的银行越能抵御传染性违约，银行体系的弹性就越强，这种影响是非线性的。第二，连通度的影响是非单调的，即最初连通度的小幅增加会增加传染效应；但在达到一定的阈值后，连通性可提高银行系统吸收冲击的能力。第三，银行间负债的规模往往会增加连锁违约的风险，即使银行持有此类风险敞口的资本。第四，在其他条件相同的情况下，更集中的银行系统显示出容易出现更大的系统风险。在对主要分析的扩展中，我们研究了流动性效应如何与银行结构相互作用以产生更大的系统崩溃机会。我们最终考虑传染的风险如何取决于组织结构固有的不对称程度（分层）
