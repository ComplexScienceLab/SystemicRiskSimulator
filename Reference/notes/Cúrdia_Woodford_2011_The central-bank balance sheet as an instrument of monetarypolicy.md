### Abstract

 We extend a standard New Keynesian model to allow an analysis of ‘‘unconventional’’ dimensions of policy alongside traditional interest-rate policy. We ﬁnd that quantitative easing in the strict sense is likely to be ineffective, but that targeted asset purchases by a central bank can instead be effective when ﬁnancial markets are sufﬁciently disrupted, and we discuss the conditions under which such interventions increase welfare. We also discuss optimal policy with regard to the payment of interest on reserves.

# 摘要

我们扩展了标准的新凯恩斯主义模型，可以对政策的“非常规”维度与传统利率政策进行分析。我们发现严格意义上的量化宽松可能无效，但是，当金融市场受到充分破坏时，由中央银行进行有针对性的资产购买可能反而有效，并且我们讨论了在何种条件下此类干预措施可以增加福利。我们还讨论了有关支付储备金利息的最佳政策。
